How it works

Four steps from application to cash.

  1. 1. Apply

    Fill in the short form below with your details.

  2. 2. Send documents

    Email your ID, latest payslip and 3 months of bank statements.

  3. 3. We assess

    With your consent, we check your credit record and affordability.

  4. 4. Get paid

    If approved, you sign your agreement and the money is paid to your bank account.

What you need

  • A valid South African ID
  • Your latest payslip or proof of income
  • 3 months of bank statements
  • Your active bank account details
Stay safe: MMAL will never ask you to pay a fee before your loan is paid out. If someone does, it is not us.

After sending, attach your ID, payslip and bank statements to the same email. We reply within 1 business day.

Common questions

Do I pay anything to apply?

No. Applying is free, and we never charge fees before paying out a loan.

Will you check my credit record?

Yes, but only with your permission, which you give by ticking the box on the application form.

How fast will I hear back?

We aim to respond within 1 business day once we have all your documents.

Is my information safe?

We use your details only to assess your application and handle them in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).
